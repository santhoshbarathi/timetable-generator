Enforced Scheduling Invariants

  • ✓
    Compulsory Faculty Workload (16 Hours/Week):

    All faculty members have a mandatory compulsory teaching schedule of 16 hours per week.

  • ✓
    Zero Free Period Invariant:

    No free periods allowed in any class schedule. All 40 slots across Periods 1 to 8 (Monday–Friday) are 100% scheduled.

  • ✓
    Single Booking Invariant:

    No faculty member is assigned to two different classes in the same period.

  • ✓
    Fixed Lunch Break (12:30 PM - 01:05 PM):

    35-minute midday break occurs consistently after Period 4 across all classes.

  • ✓
    Automatic Short Break Placement:

    Morning break placed automatically after Period 2; afternoon tea break after Period 6.

  • ✓
    Part-Time Fixed Half-Day Window:

    Part-time faculty only scheduled in Periods 1–4 (09:00 - 12:30). Strict prohibition against afternoon scheduling.

  • ✓
    Daily Subject Frequency Constraint:

    Each subject receives exactly 1 period per class per day (no same-day duplicates).

  • ✓
    Weekly Subject Target Honor:

    Every subject's configured weekly periods are exactly matched in the generated timetable.
